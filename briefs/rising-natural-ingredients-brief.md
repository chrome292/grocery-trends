# Rising natural ingredients: what published data shows

For the Presence team. Sources opened 22 November 2026. Research only.

Published national data supports four of the trend radar's tags with a growth figure, names three more without one, and says nothing local. No source I opened covers Maryland, DC or Virginia, or any single store. That local view is what the radar is meant to add.

## What the numbers say

**Functional mushrooms are the best documented.** SPINS reports functional mushroom sales of $494.7 million for the 52 weeks ending 6 September 2026 across Natural, Conventional Multi-Outlet and Amazon, up 23.2% in dollars and 34.8% in units. Units grew faster than dollars. In a SPINS survey of 1,000 vitamin and supplement consumers in May 2025, 16% had bought functional mushrooms in the past 12 months and 35% said they were interested in trying or buying them. This is the whole functional mushroom category. The article gives no figure for mushroom coffee.

**That unit growth stands out against the wider market.** Patrick Sheridan, President and CEO of INFRA, writes that "in the first quarter of 2026, unit sales flattened or declined across retail channels" while dollar sales stayed positive, citing SPINS (New Hope Network, 12 August 2026). The two periods differ, so this is context and not a like for like comparison.

**Ashwagandha, colostrum and sea moss have growth figures, but weaker ones.** All come from one interview with Scott Dicker of SPINS, where the interviewer read the figures out and the page gives no period for most of them:

| Ingredient | Figure as printed | Base as printed | Period |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ashwagandha | 27% sales growth | multi-outlet | 2025 |
| Colostrum | over 2,000% | hair, skin and nail category, multi-outlet | not stated |
| Colostrum | over 200% | immune health category, natural channel | not stated |
| Sea moss | 204% sales growth | channel not tied to the figure | not stated |
| Sea moss | 358% sales growth | immune health category | not stated |

Treat these as directional. Very large percentages usually mean a small starting base, and the page does not say whether they count dollars or units. On colostrum, Scott Dicker's own reply was "Not 2,000% year-over-year, but still big potential growth."

**Whole Foods Market's buyers point the same way on beauty from within.** Its 8 trend predictions for 2027 include one called Beautrition: "Glowing skin is becoming an inside job." The products listed under it include sea moss, collagen and bone broth. A matcha granola appears under its Japanese comfort food trend. These are predictions by its Trends Council, not sales data.
